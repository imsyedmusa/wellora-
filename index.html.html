FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Wellora free?

Yes. The free plan includes core tracking and a starter library. Plus unlocks personalization and the full content library.

Which devices does Wellora support?

iPhone (iOS 16+) and Android (10+), with Apple Watch and Wear OS companions.

Does it work with my wearable?

Yes. Wellora syncs with Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, Oura, and more.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. Your data is encrypted and never sold. You control what's shared.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, in a couple of taps from your account settings.

Is Wellora medical advice?

No. Wellora supports healthy habits but is not a substitute for a doctor or healthcare professional.
